# Revenue-share framework (draft)

> Living document. Nothing below is a binding offer; it is the framework we
> intend to sign with listed contributors when SokoFlow first earns money.
> This project has no revenue today and is pre-registration.

## Principles

1. **SokoFlow stays open source.** Commercial income comes from hosting,
   support, certification, and deployment work — not from selling the core.
2. **Credit first, money second.** Every contributor on
   [CONTRIBUTORS.md](../CONTRIBUTORS.md) is credited permanently, regardless of
   revenue.
3. **Written and auditable.** When the first commercial revenue exists, a
   one-page agreement is signed with each listed module lead and recorded in
   this repository's history.
4. **Simple enough to audit.** No revenue pools, no percentages of percentages.
   One number per module, computed from net revenue.

## Definitions (target)

- **Module lead** — a person listed in CONTRIBUTORS.md as owning a module.
- **Net revenue** — revenue from hosting, support, certification, and
  deployment, minus taxes and directly attributable payment-processing fees.
- **Module share** — a defined percentage of net revenue attributable to the
  corridor or deliverable the module powers.

## Committed commitments (when revenue first exists)

1. We sign a one-page agreement with each listed module lead.
2. The share is a single, fixed percentage, discussable but never retroactively
   reduced.
3. The share is paid on net revenue from what the module powers, on a defined
   schedule, once it crosses a minimum threshold.
4. Every payment is visible in the repository history.

## Open questions (to be settled with the first contributors)

- What percentage is fair and meaningful for a module lead?
- Minimum threshold before the first payment is owed?
- How "attributable to the module" is measured per corridor?
